# Code of conduct

Marteloio is a small project made for sellers, many of whom are new to software and to open
source. Everyone who takes part (in issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, or anywhere
else the project is discussed) is asked to follow this code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or not technical.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person, and say what would make it better.
- Accept that people disagree, and let the maintainer make the final call on the project.
- Protect privacy: don't post anyone's personal details, keys, account data or listings
  without their permission, and remove your own before you share logs or screenshots.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Jokes or remarks that put people down for their identity, background, age, body or abilities.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, scams, or links to unofficial "Marteloio" downloads.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider unwelcoming in a project like this.

## Reporting a problem

Report it privately. On this repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**
and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainer can read these reports, and they are
kept confidential. Please include links or screenshots, if you have them.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report and decides what to do. That can be a private note, a public
warning, editing or removing comments, issues or code, or blocking someone from the project for
a time or for good. The aim is to keep the project safe and welcoming, not to punish.

## Where this applies

In every project space (the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and the
website), and whenever someone represents the project in public.
