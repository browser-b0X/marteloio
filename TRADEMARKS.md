# Marteloio name and logo

Marteloio's source code is free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can
use, change and share the code, including in your own fork or product.

The **Marteloio name, the Marteloio logo and symbol, and the "Details, drafted." tagline**
are the project's brand and are **not** licensed under the MIT License. They are reserved
by browser-b0X. This covers the files in `assets/brand/`, the favicon, header and social-preview images
in `docs/assets/`, and any name or logo close enough to be mistaken for them.

## Why

People install Marteloio on their own computers and give it their AI and eBay keys. If a
modified copy could call itself Marteloio, someone could add harmful code and pass it off
as this project. Keeping the name for the official project lets people tell the real thing
from a fork.

## What's fine without asking

- Saying that your project is "based on Marteloio" or "a fork of Marteloio", in plain text,
  as long as it's clear it is not the official project.
- Writing about Marteloio, reviewing it, or linking to this repository, including showing
  the logo next to that coverage.
- Keeping the name and logo in an unmodified copy of this repository, or in a fork that
  exists only to send changes back here.

## What needs a different name

If you publish or share a modified version (a fork, a repackaged download, a hosted copy):

- Give it a different name that isn't confusingly similar to Marteloio.
- Replace the logo, symbol and favicon with your own.
- Change or remove the "Listing created with Marteloio" credit that the app adds to
  generated listings, so buyers aren't told your version is Marteloio.
- Don't suggest that browser-b0X made, reviewed or endorses your version.

## Official sources

The only official sources are this repository,
[github.com/browser-b0X/marteloio](https://github.com/browser-b0X/marteloio), and its website,
[browser-b0x.github.io/marteloio](https://browser-b0x.github.io/marteloio/). Copies offered
anywhere else under the Marteloio name are not from this project.

To ask about using the name or logo in another way, open an issue or a discussion in this
repository.
