How it works

Four stops from a pile of notes to a listing.

Write what you know about the item the way you would say it out loud. Marteloio does the wording and the layout; you keep the facts.

  1. 1

    Describe

    Type the model, what's included, what you tested and every flaw. Pick the condition yourself.

  2. 2

    Generate

    One request drafts a title, description, item specifics and highlights. If one AI service is busy, the next one answers.

  3. 3

    Review

    Check the title against the 80-character ruler, edit any section as plain text, or regenerate just one part.

  4. 4

    Copy

    Copy the HTML into eBay's description editor. Preview it at desktop and phone width first.

Features

Less typing, fewer AI calls, nothing made up.

Built to take the friction out of the parts of a listing that are the same every time, and to stay out of the way of the parts only you know.

eBay-ready output

Listings that look the part on desktop and phone.

Every layout is self-contained HTML that eBay's editor accepts: no scripts, no outside links, no web fonts, and a plain-text mobile summary at the top. Pick a layout or press Shuffle look for a fresh style.

  • Highlights, specifics, condition and what's included, laid out for you
  • Edit the HTML directly, or edit the text and let Marteloio rebuild it
  • Copy HTML, copy the title, or download the file
Bundles and lots

Bundles that fill themselves in.

Describe the lot once. Each line becomes an item card and a What's included chip, with no AI request. Filled cards fold to a single line; brands are collected for you.

Reference lookup

Real specs, only when you accept them.

Search other sellers' eBay listings by name or barcode, review the specifications they list, and accept one as a labelled reference. Nothing is filled in behind your back.

  • A Get set up guide that ticks itself off until your first listing is in eBay
  • A tip beside every field, and Help (?) whenever you want the tour again

Templates

Sell it again without asking the AI again.

Save a finished listing as a template. When you start a similar item, Marteloio suggests it, and Regenerate rewrites one section with a short request instead of a full one.

Honest by design

Condition comes from you.

The AI writes the words; it never decides the facts.

  • Untested stays untested; For parts is never upgraded
  • Every flaw you mention stays in the listing
  • No condition picked means none is claimed
  • Your own edits always win over anything filled in

Local first

Your listings, your keys, your computer.

There is no Marteloio account or server. The app is a small local program you start yourself, and it only talks to the services you set up.

Stays on your PC

  • Drafts, recent history and templates, in your browser
  • AI and eBay keys, encrypted for your Windows account
  • The app itself, served only to your own machine

Sent when you ask

  • Your notes and accepted references, to the AI service you set up
  • Product names or barcodes, to eBay when you choose Look up or Find keywords

Never

  • No sign-up, tracking or analytics
  • No listing is ever published for you
  • Keys are never written to your browser storage
Install

Install in three steps.

Marteloio-Setup.exe brings everything Marteloio needs, its own Python included. It installs for your Windows account only, without administrator rights. Windows may warn you twice on the way; here is what to click.

  1. 1Download

    Get Marteloio-Setup.exe. If your browser says it isn't commonly downloaded, keep it. In Edge: … › Keep › Show more › Keep anyway.

  2. 2Open it

    Windows may show Windows protected your PC, because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3Install

    Tick or untick the desktop shortcut and choose Install. On the last page choose Finish: Marteloio opens in your browser with a short welcome setup.

Download for Windows

Free · Windows 10 and 11 · about 12 MB. The installer isn't code-signed yet; signing through the SignPath Foundation, which signs open-source projects for free, is in progress (Code signing policy). To check your download: in PowerShell, Get-FileHash .\Marteloio-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256 should show the same value as Marteloio-Setup.exe.sha256.

Then connect a free AI service.

The welcome setup asks for one key. Paste it, and Marteloio recognises the service and tests it. One is enough; a second one is a backup when the first is busy.

Google Gemini easiest, about 2 minutes → Groq Groq console → OpenRouter free models only → FreeLLMAPI runs on your PC; Marteloio finds it →

Free tiers have limits set by each service. Next time, open Marteloio from the Start menu or the desktop. To update, run a newer Marteloio-Setup.exe: your keys and drafts stay. The user guide covers the rest.

Mac or Linux? Run from source
# Needs Python 3.10 or newer, nothing else
$ git clone https://github.com/browser-b0X/marteloio.git
$ cd marteloio

# macOS / Linux (not yet verified)
$ bash start-all.sh

# Windows, without the installer
> start-all.bat
Your browser opens at localhost:8080. Stop it with Settings › About › Quit Marteloio. To use another port, set PORT in .env.
Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Marteloio is MIT-licensed open source with no paid tier. The AI comes from your own keys: Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter each offer a free tier with limits they set, and FreeLLMAPI spreads requests over many free services. Templates and single-section regeneration help you stay inside them.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

Marteloio-Setup.exe isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't know who made it and shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. Signing through the SignPath Foundation, which signs open-source projects for free, is in progress; see the code signing policy. Until then you can check the file's SHA-256 against the one on the release page.

How do I update or uninstall it?

To update, download the newer Marteloio-Setup.exe and run it. It closes Marteloio, replaces the program and keeps your keys, settings and drafts. To uninstall, open Windows Settings › Apps, choose Marteloio, then Uninstall. It asks whether to remove your saved keys and settings too; the default keeps them.

Does it post listings to eBay for me?

No. Marteloio drafts the title and the description HTML. You copy them into eBay's listing form, check the preview, and publish yourself.

Do I need an eBay developer account?

Only for Look up and Find keywords, which search other sellers' listings through eBay's Browse API. A free eBay Developers account gives you the keys. Everything else works without it.

Does it work on a Mac or Linux?

The installer is for Windows 10 and 11. On a Mac or Linux you can run Marteloio from source with Python 3.10 or newer: bash start-all.sh starts it. That launcher is included but not yet verified, so please report what you find.

What is the small credit at the bottom of a listing?

Generated listings end with a short "Listing created with Marteloio" line. It follows eBay's rules for third-party credits: ten words or fewer, no links and no web address. It's how a free tool gets found; it is plain HTML, so you can remove it if you prefer.

How do I know I have the real Marteloio?

Download it only from github.com/browser-b0X/marteloio or this site; the installer is published only on the repository's Releases page, with its SHA-256. The code and logo files are MIT-licensed, so anyone may build on them, but the Marteloio name and logo are the project's trademarks: a modified copy has to use its own name and logo. Treat a "Marteloio" download from anywhere else as untrusted and don't give it your keys.

Can the AI get things wrong?

Yes, which is why Marteloio never decides condition or flaws, keeps references labelled, and asks you to review before copying. Treat every draft as a draft.
